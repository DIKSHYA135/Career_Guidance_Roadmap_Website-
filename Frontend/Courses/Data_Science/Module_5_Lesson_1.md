## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even experienced developers make these mistakes with Unsupervised Learning. Learn from them now so you do not repeat them:

❌ **Mistake 1: Skipping the theory**
Many beginners want to jump straight to code without understanding *why* Unsupervised Learning works the way it does. This leads to fragile implementations that break under edge cases.

❌ **Mistake 2: Over-engineering**
Just because Unsupervised Learning *can* be complex does not mean your implementation should be. Always prefer the simplest solution that satisfies your requirements.

❌ **Mistake 3: Ignoring documentation**
Every technology related to Unsupervised Learning has official documentation. Make reading official docs a habit — it is the fastest way to get accurate, up-to-date information.
